WoW PvP Editor

A personal desktop tool by Arxgen.

WoW PvP Editor is a personal tool that runs only on its owner's computer. It turns the owner's own World of Warcraft PvP recordings into short videos and helps publish them to the owner's own channels: YouTube (@arxgenwow), Instagram (@arxgen09), the Arxgen Facebook Page and TikTok.

What it does

  • Edits the owner's own gameplay recordings into vertical and horizontal videos.
  • Uploads the finished videos to the owner's own accounts, privately or scheduled, only after the owner confirms each one.
  • Reads basic information about the owner's own channel (name and the status of the uploaded videos).

Who can use it

Only its owner. It is not a public service: there are no other users, no sign-ups and no accounts of other people.
